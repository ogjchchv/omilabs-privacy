Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Omilabs Uploader application ("the App") collects, uses, and handles your information when you use our service.

1. What the App Does

Omilabs Uploader is a private internal tool used by Omkar Ghate (Omilabs) to upload videos, thumbnails, captions, and metadata to the Omilabs YouTube channel via the YouTube Data API v3.

2. Data We Access

The App requests access to your YouTube account solely to:

  • Upload video files to the Omilabs YouTube channel
  • Set video titles, descriptions, tags, and thumbnails
  • Upload caption/subtitle files (SRT format)
  • Add videos to playlists
  • Schedule video publish times

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

The App does not:

  • Collect, store, or share any personal data from YouTube users or viewers
  • Access any YouTube account other than the Omilabs channel owner's account
  • Sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties
  • Use data for advertising or analytics purposes

4. OAuth Tokens

The App uses Google OAuth 2.0 to authenticate. OAuth tokens are stored locally in an encrypted format and are never shared, logged, or transmitted to any third party. Tokens are used exclusively to make authorized API calls to YouTube on behalf of the channel owner.

5. Google API Services

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data Retention

No user data is retained by the App beyond what YouTube itself stores as a result of the upload (video files, metadata, captions). OAuth tokens can be revoked at any time via Google Account Permissions.
